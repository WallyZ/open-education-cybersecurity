# Cybersecurity Assessment Bank

CYB-101 favors essays, memos, simulations, and defended projects because cybersecurity mastery requires synthesis, ethics, and transfer.

## Assessment Types

- Diagnostic essay: explain a security failure from assets, incentives, controls, and evidence.
- Threat model: identify assets, actors, entry points, trust boundaries, threats, mitigations, and assumptions.
- Risk memo: compare mitigations, tradeoffs, cost, residual risk, and communication needs.
- Synthetic log triage: identify suspicious patterns without overclaiming evidence.
- Incident tabletop: write first-hour response steps that preserve evidence and reduce harm.
- Capstone defense: defend and revise a security improvement plan.

## Rubric

Strong work:

- cites objective IDs and scenario evidence
- separates evidence from suspicion
- proposes controls that match the threat model
- states residual risk and tradeoffs
- respects authorization and privacy boundaries
- revises after feedback

Weak work:

- relies on tool names without reasoning
- blames users without system analysis
- claims certainty not supported by evidence
- recommends unauthorized testing
- ignores recovery, communication, or human impact
